## Product Context

**Product Name:** RecallWire
**Company:** RecallWire
**Industry:** Healthcare / Medical Device Recall Management
**Type:** SaaS

**One-Line Description:**
> AI-powered medical device recall management software for healthcare facilities.

**Value Proposition:**
> Respond to recalls 10x faster with complete compliance documentation.

**Problem Statement:**
> Healthcare facilities receive 150,000+ FDA alerts annually. Only 2-5% apply to their inventory. Critical recalls get buried in noise, while manual response takes 20-40 hours per recall with documentation scattered across emails and spreadsheets.

**Solution Statement:**
> RecallWire automatically matches FDA recalls to your specific inventory, guides your team through resolution, and generates audit-ready documentation—reducing recall response time by 10x.
