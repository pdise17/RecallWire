## Core Messaging Blocks

Use these interchangeably across any asset:

### Tagline
> Respond to recalls 10x faster with complete compliance documentation

### Problem Statement (Short)
> Healthcare facilities receive 150,000+ FDA alerts annually. Only 2-5% apply to their inventory. Critical recalls get buried in noise.

### Problem Statement (Long)
> Managing medical device recalls manually means 20-40 hours per recall response, documentation scattered across emails and spreadsheets, and significant patient safety risk when recalls slip through the cracks.

### Solution Statement
> RecallWire automatically matches FDA recalls to your specific inventory, guides your team through resolution, and generates audit-ready documentation—reducing recall response time by 10x.

### Three-Step Summary
> **Detect** real-time FDA monitoring | **Match** AI-powered inventory matching | **Resolve** guided workflows with auto-documentation

## Visual Style Notes

- **Icons:** Line-style, consistent stroke weight, modern flat
- **Imagery:** Abstract tech patterns, clean healthcare environments—avoid cliché stock photos
- **Mockups:** Use device frames (laptop/monitor) for platform visuals
- **Tone:** Professional, urgent but not alarmist, data-driven

---

## Logo Usage

**Logo Style:** Clean wordmark "RECALLWIRE" stacked as "RECALL" / "WIRE"
- The "A" in RECALL and "I" in WIRE are highlighted in brand teal
- Primary: Black text with teal accents (for light backgrounds)
- Reversed: White text with teal accents (for dark backgrounds)
- Minimum clear space: height of the "R" on all sides
- Never stretch, rotate, or add effects
