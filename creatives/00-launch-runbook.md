# RecallWire Launch Runbook

**Objective:** Get RecallWire to a supremely presentable state for aggressive outbound campaigns.

**Philosophy:** Website is the foundation. Everything points there. Lock that first, then arm sales, then activate demand gen.

---

## Executive Summary

| Phase | Focus | Duration | Gate |
|-------|-------|----------|------|
| **0** | Foundation | Week 1-2 | Website live with pricing |
| **1** | Sales Enablement | Week 2-3 | Sales team can demo + leave-behind |
| **2** | Demand Gen Assets | Week 3-4 | LinkedIn presence + content queued |
| **3** | Campaign Activation | Week 4+ | Hot recall triggers armed |

---
