## Design Notes (All Versions)

- **White space:** Don't overcrowd—this is a leave-behind, not a data sheet
- **Icons:** Use sparingly, consistent style
- **Stats:** Large numbers (32pt+), small labels
- **Callout boxes:** Light teal background, subtle border
- **Print test:** Ensure readable in grayscale
