## Series 2: Recall of the Week

**Frequency:** Weekly (Mondays)
**Purpose:** Newsjacking, demonstrate expertise, provide value

### Template Design
- Header: "RECALL ALERT" + week/date
- FDA recall classification badge (Class I/II/III)
- Product name and manufacturer
- "What to know" bullet points (2-3)
- RecallWire logo + "Follow for weekly updates"

### Content Framework
```
🚨 RECALL OF THE WEEK

[Product Name] — [Manufacturer]
Classification: Class [I/II]

What facilities need to know:
• [Key risk/issue]
• [Affected lot numbers or scope]
• [Recommended action]

Is this recall in your inventory?
RecallWire customers know in seconds.

#MedicalDevices #PatientSafety #HealthcareCompliance
```

## Series 3: Before/After

**Frequency:** 2x per month
**Purpose:** Show transformation, drive demos

### Template Design (Carousel - 3 slides)

**Slide 1:** "Before RecallWire"
- Visual: Cluttered desk, overflowing inbox, spreadsheets
- Stat: "40 hours per recall"
- Tone: Frustrated, chaotic

**Slide 2:** "After RecallWire"
- Visual: Clean dashboard mockup, organized workflow
- Stat: "4 hours per recall"
- Tone: Calm, controlled

**Slide 3:** CTA
- "Ready to transform your recall process?"
- "Link in bio" or "DM us for a demo"

## Series 4: Did You Know?

**Frequency:** 2x per week
**Purpose:** Education, thought leadership

### Template Design
- "DID YOU KNOW?" header
- Key fact in large text
- Source citation (small, bottom)
- RecallWire logo

### Content Examples

**Post 1:**
"DID YOU KNOW? The average hospital receives 100-150 FDA recall alerts every month. Only 40% actually apply to their inventory."

**Post 2:**
"DID YOU KNOW? Joint Commission surveyors can request recall documentation going back 3+ years. Can your team produce it?"

**Post 3:**
"DID YOU KNOW? Software-related recalls now account for 35% of all FDA medical device recalls—and growing."

**Post 4:**
"DID YOU KNOW? Most legacy recall systems were built in 2004. That's before the iPhone existed."

**Post 5:**
"DID YOU KNOW? Class I recalls have a 'reasonable probability of serious adverse health consequences or death.' There were 75+ in 2024."
