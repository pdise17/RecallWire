Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "location"?
A location is any distinct facility where you manage inventory and respond to recalls—hospitals, clinics, surgery centers, imaging centers, etc. Multiple departments within one facility count as one location.
Can I upgrade my plan anytime?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately, and we'll prorate the difference. Moving from Standard to Enterprise triggers a sales conversation to ensure the right fit for your organization.
What happens if I exceed my RecallWire Assistant queries?
We'll notify you when you reach 80% of your limit. If you exceed your limit, you can purchase additional query packs ($49 for 100 queries) or upgrade to Enterprise for higher limits or unlimited access.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or government?
Yes. Contact us for special pricing for qualifying organizations including nonprofits, government facilities, and academic institutions.
What's included in implementation?
Standard: Self-serve onboarding with help center, video guides, and an onboarding call.

Enterprise: White-glove implementation with dedicated team, training sessions, custom configuration, and ongoing support.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. Start a 14-day free trial of the Standard plan. No credit card required.
How does RecallWire compare to ECRI?
ECRI has unmatched expertise—50 years in patient safety. But their Alerts Workflow alone costs $75,878/year (verified). RecallWire delivers AI-powered recall management at $45,000-$60,000 for comparable deployments, saving you $16,000-$31,000 annually (21-41% less). Same audit trail, same compliance, plus AI guidance ECRI doesn't offer.
How is RecallWire different from PAR Excellence?
PAR Excellence (~$2,400/year) tracks at the task level—that you received and acknowledged a recall. RecallWire Standard ($4,999/year) tracks at the Recall Action level—what you did with the affected inventory (quarantine, return, destroy, correct) with guided workflows, role-based sign-offs, and complete documentation. For an extra $200/month, you get completely different audit protection.
What payment options are available?
Standard: Annual ($4,999/year) or monthly ($449/month). Annual plans billed annually; monthly plans billed monthly with no long-term commitment.

Enterprise: Annual billing with flexible payment terms. Multi-year discounts available.
