## Discovery Questions

Ask these to uncover pain points with OneRecall or openness to alternatives:

1. **"How long did your OneRecall implementation take?"** (Often 6+ months)
2. **"When was the last time you saw a significant platform update?"** (Legacy systems update slowly)
3. **"How do you handle recall management for smaller facilities that don't have OneRecall?"** (They often don't serve SMB)
4. **"What does your OneRecall contract renewal process look like?"** (Often opaque, long)
5. **"If you could change one thing about OneRecall, what would it be?"** (UX complaints common)
6. **"Do you have dedicated staff for recall management, or is it a shared responsibility?"** (RMaaS opportunity)

## Objection Handling

### "OneRecall is the industry standard"

> "They are—60% market share. But 'standard' doesn't mean 'best.' They built their platform in 2004. The healthcare landscape has changed dramatically. RecallWire gives you modern AI, transparent pricing, and faster implementation. Many organizations are realizing that the industry standard is due for an upgrade."

### "We've used OneRecall for years"

> "That tenure is valuable—you know recall management. But it also means you've been on a platform that's 20 years old. When did you last see a major innovation from them? RecallWire brings AI-powered guidance, modern UX, and managed services they don't offer. And we make switching easy—our implementation team handles the transition."

### "Our whole system uses OneRecall"

> "Enterprise-wide deployments are our specialty too. We work with health systems of all sizes. The question isn't 'can you switch?'—it's 'what would be better?' Let me show you what modern recall management looks like, and you can decide if it's worth exploring."

### "OneRecall has rapidID for AI matching"

> "rapidID is their matching algorithm—it's good at identifying which products are affected. But RecallWire Assistant goes further: it guides your team through what to DO about it. When an alert comes in, our AI explains the recall, recommends actions, and walks through the resolution workflow. That's the difference between matching and guidance."

### "We need the Johns Hopkins credibility"

> "Johns Hopkins is impressive. But you're not buying Johns Hopkins—you're buying Inmar's 20-year-old platform. RecallWire delivers the same compliance outcomes with better technology. We'd be happy to connect you with our enterprise customers for references."
