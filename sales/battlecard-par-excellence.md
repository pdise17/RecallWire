## The Critical Gap: Task Tracking vs. Recall Action Tracking

**THIS IS THE KEY DIFFERENTIATOR**

PAR Excellence tracks at the **task level** (communication status), but does **NOT** track **Recall Actions** (what you do with affected inventory).

### What PAR Excellence Tracks (Task Level)

- Did you receive the notification? ✓
- Did you acknowledge it? ✓
- Did you respond to the manufacturer? ✓

### What PAR Excellence Does NOT Track (Recall Action Level)

- What did you DO with the affected inventory?
- Quarantine status and location
- Return to manufacturer (with shipping documentation)
- Destruction/disposal (with verification)
- Correction/repair actions
- Patient notification (for implanted devices)
- Role-based sign-offs and approvals

## Why This Gap Matters: FDA Requirements

Per [FDA 21 CFR Part 806](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/recalls-corrections-and-removals-devices), healthcare facilities must document specific recall actions:

| FDA Recall Action | Documentation Required | PAR Tracks? | RecallWire Tracks? |
|-------------------|----------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Quarantine** | Location, quantity, date, who | No | **Yes** |
| **Return** | Shipping records, quantities, dates | No | **Yes** |
| **Destroy** | Destruction verification, witness | No | **Yes** |
| **Correct** | Action taken, by whom, verification | No | **Yes** |
| **Patient Monitoring** | Patient list, notifications, follow-up | No | **Yes** |

> "A request for termination of a recall order shall include **a description of the disposition of the recalled device**."
> — FDA 21 CFR Part 810

## Objection Handling

### "PAR Excellence is cheaper"

> "PAR is $2,400/year. RecallWire is $4,999—about $200 more per month. But here's the critical difference: PAR doesn't track what you DO with recalled items. No quarantine documentation, no return verification, no sign-offs. You're saving $2,600/year while taking on $40,000+ in audit risk. One Joint Commission finding costs more than 15 years of that difference. Is that a bet worth making?"

### "Baptist Health passed Joint Commission with NotiSphere"

> "That's great for Baptist Health. But look at what they presented: their internal processes, their manual documentation, their recall coordinator's expertise. NotiSphere helped with notifications—but Baptist Health built their own compliance documentation on top of it. RecallWire includes that documentation built-in. You don't have to create it yourself."

### "Mayo Clinic uses PAR"

> "Mayo is an exceptional organization with exceptional resources. They have dedicated recall teams, established processes, and compliance infrastructure. The question is: do you? For most facilities, you need a solution that guides the workflow, not just notifies. That's what RecallWire does."

### "We just need notifications, not workflow"

> "Notifications are table stakes—everyone does that. The hard part is what happens next. When Joint Commission asks 'what did you do with these recalled items?', a notification acknowledgement isn't enough. You need documented actions: quarantine, return, destroy. RecallWire captures all of that. PAR doesn't."

### "PAR is simpler and easier"

> "Simple to use, yes. But simple isn't the same as complete. PAR is simple because it does less—it stops at notification. RecallWire is designed to be just as easy to use, but it continues through the entire recall lifecycle. The guided workflow actually makes it easier to do the right thing."

### "We're already using PAR"

> "That gives you a head start—your team understands recall management. The question is whether acknowledgement-only tracking is enough. When's your next Joint Commission survey? Let me show you what a complete audit report looks like with RecallWire. If PAR can generate the same thing, stay with them. If not, we should talk."
