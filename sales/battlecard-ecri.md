## Discovery Questions

Ask these to uncover pain points with ECRI or openness to alternatives:

1. **"What's your total ECRI spend across all products?"** (Often $200K+ for full suite)
2. **"How often do you actually talk to your Recall Advisor?"** (Often underutilized)
3. **"Are you using Capital Guide and Supply Guide, or just Alerts?"** (If just Alerts, they're overpaying)
4. **"How accessible is support outside business hours?"** (Human advisor = limited hours)
5. **"What would happen if you could get 80% of the value for 40% of the cost?"** (Opens ROI conversation)
6. **"Do you have smaller facilities that aren't covered by ECRI?"** (SMB opportunity)

## Objection Handling

### "ECRI is the gold standard for patient safety"

> "Absolutely—50 years of expertise is impressive. But at $76K for Alerts Workflow alone, you're paying a significant premium for that reputation. RecallWire delivers equivalent recall coverage at $45-60K—that's $16-31K in annual savings. Same audit trail, same compliance outcomes, with AI-powered guidance ECRI doesn't offer. The question is: is the ECRI name worth $16-31K per year?"

### "We need ECRI's early alerts (ahead of FDA)"

> "That's valuable for certain high-risk recalls. But let's be honest: how many recalls per year truly require hours-ahead notification? For the vast majority, same-day is sufficient. RecallWire monitors FDA in real-time. And for those critical situations, our RMaaS team provides emergency response support that ECRI doesn't offer."

### "We get a dedicated Recall Advisor with ECRI"

> "Recall Advisor is great—during business hours. RecallWire Assistant is available 24/7, provides instant guidance, and can handle unlimited queries. Plus, with RMaaS, you get a whole team, not just one advisor. And our AI doesn't take vacation or change jobs."

### "ECRI is nonprofit, so we trust them more"

> "We respect ECRI's nonprofit mission. But nonprofit doesn't mean less expensive—$76K for Alerts alone proves that. RecallWire's business model is simple: we succeed when you stay compliant. Our incentives are aligned. And we're transparent about our pricing, which ECRI isn't."

### "We're already locked into ECRI's full suite"

> "Many organizations use ECRI for Capital Guide or Supply Guide and assume they need Alerts too. But you can unbundle. Use ECRI for procurement benchmarking where they excel, and use RecallWire for recall management where we excel—at 40% less cost."

### "Our procurement team wants one vendor"

> "Single-vendor simplicity is valuable. But is it worth $31K per year? RecallWire integrates with your existing systems. We're not asking you to replace ECRI entirely—just to use the right tool for each job."

## Ideal Competitive Scenarios

### When We Win

- Customer paying for full ECRI suite but only using Alerts
- Price-sensitive organizations in contract renewal
- Smaller facilities in system not covered by ECRI
- Need for 24/7 support (ECRI Advisor has limited hours)
- Want managed services ECRI doesn't offer
- Frustrated with bundling pressure

### When It's Harder

- Deep existing ECRI relationship across multiple products
- Strong trust in ECRI nonprofit brand
- Procurement locked into ECRI enterprise agreement
- Need for ECRI's specific cybersecurity alerts
- "Ahead of FDA" alerting is mission-critical requirement
