# Hot Recall Outreach Campaign

## Campaign Overview

**Concept:** Proactively reach out to healthcare facilities when high-impact recalls are announced, offering RecallWire as the solution to manage the recall response.

**Tagline Options:**
- "Have you started the recall process for [X] yet? If not, hit the RecallWire!"
- "FDA just recalled [X]. Is your facility ready?"
- "[X] Recall Alert: Are you covered?"

**Why This Works:**
- Creates urgency with real, timely events
- Demonstrates RecallWire's value at the moment of need
- Positions us as proactive partners, not reactive vendors
- Higher open/response rates due to immediate relevance

---

## Campaign Triggers

### Tier 1: High Priority (Launch within 24 hours)

| Trigger | Why High Priority |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Class I Recalls** | Life-threatening, immediate action required |
| **High-volume devices** | Affects many facilities (pacemakers, pumps, implants) |
| **Major manufacturer recalls** | Medtronic, Abbott, J&J, Boston Scientific, etc. |
| **Multi-product recalls** | Single manufacturer, multiple products |
| **Recalls with patient contact requirements** | Implanted devices requiring patient notification |

### Tier 2: Standard Priority (Launch within 48-72 hours)

| Trigger | Why Standard |
|---------|--------------|
| **Class II Recalls** | Serious but not life-threatening |
| **Specialty device recalls** | Affects specific departments (cardiology, radiology, etc.) |
| **Regional manufacturer recalls** | Smaller footprint but still impactful |

### Tier 3: Opportunistic (Weekly roundup)

| Trigger | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Multiple Class II/III recalls** | Weekly "Recall Roundup" email |
| **Trending recall categories** | "3 Cardiac Device Recalls This Month" |

## Operational Workflow

### Daily Monitoring

```
1. Monitor FDA recall feed (6am, 12pm, 6pm)
   - openFDA API or RSS
   - Set alerts for Class I immediately

2. Assess recall impact
   - Device type (broad vs. niche)
   - Manufacturer size
   - Estimated affected facilities

3. Decide campaign tier
   - Tier 1: Launch within 24 hours
   - Tier 2: Launch within 48-72 hours
   - Tier 3: Include in weekly roundup
```

### Campaign Launch Checklist

```
□ Recall details confirmed (FDA source)
□ Email copy customized with recall specifics
□ Subject line A/B test set up
□ Target list pulled (by facility type, region, role)
□ Landing page live (if Tier 1)
□ LinkedIn posts scheduled
□ Google Ads keywords added (if Tier 1)
□ CRM campaign created for tracking
□ Team notified of incoming leads
```
