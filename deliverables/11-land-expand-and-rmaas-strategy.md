# RecallWire: Land & Expand Strategy + RMaaS Offering

## Executive Summary

This document outlines two strategic initiatives:

1. **Land & Expand Strategy** - Get customers in at competitive entry pricing, then grow the relationship through natural upgrade triggers and upsell opportunities

2. **Recall Management as a Service (RMaaS)** - A differentiated managed service offering where RecallWire handles recall operations on behalf of customers

**Key finding:** Managed recall services exist for device *manufacturers* (IQVIA, Sedgwick, Quest), but **no competitor offers managed services to healthcare facilities** (the receiving end of recalls). This is a significant whitespace opportunity.

## Part 2: Recall Management as a Service (RMaaS)

### Market Opportunity

#### Competitive Landscape

| Provider | Target Customer | Service Type |
|----------|-----------------|--------------|
| IQVIA MedTech | Device manufacturers | Recall execution (outbound) |
| Sedgwick | Device manufacturers | Recall logistics & notifications |
| Quest International | Device manufacturers | Field corrective actions |
| ECRI | Healthcare facilities | Software only (no managed service) |
| Inmar OneRecall | Healthcare facilities | Software only (no managed service) |
| PAR/NotiSphere | Healthcare facilities | Software only (no managed service) |
| **RecallWire** | Healthcare facilities | **Software + Managed Service** |

**Key insight:** Managed services exist for the *supply side* (manufacturers executing recalls) but **not for the *demand side*** (hospitals receiving and responding to recalls). RecallWire can own this whitespace.

#### Why Healthcare Facilities Need RMaaS

| Pain Point | Evidence |
|------------|----------|
| **Staffing shortages** | Healthcare facing critical workforce gaps |
| **Expertise gaps** | Recall compliance requires specialized knowledge |
| **Audit anxiety** | Joint Commission, state, CMS inspections |
| **Time constraints** | Clinical staff pulled into recall response |
| **Inconsistent processes** | Lack of standardization across facilities |
| **Turnover risk** | Knowledge walks out the door with departing staff |

#### Target Customers for RMaaS

| Segment | Why RMaaS | Willingness to Pay |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| Small hospitals (<100 beds) | No dedicated recall staff | Medium |
| Rural/critical access hospitals | Limited resources | Medium-High |
| ASCs & imaging centers | Focus on clinical, not compliance | High |
| Health systems during transitions | M&A, staff turnover, new facilities | High |
| Facilities with compliance issues | Recent citations, audit failures | Very High |

### RMaaS Service Tiers

#### Tier 1: RMaaS Essentials ($499/month)

**What we do:**
- Daily alert triage and prioritization
- Weekly summary reports
- Quarterly compliance reviews
- Audit-ready documentation maintenance

**What customer does:**
- Physical inventory actions (quarantine, removal)
- Final sign-offs on resolutions

**Best for:** Small facilities wanting to offload triage burden

**Margin profile:** ~70% (mostly automation + periodic human review)

---

#### Tier 2: RMaaS Core ($999/month)

**What we do:**
Everything in Essentials, plus:
- Active recall response management
- Coordination of resolution workflows
- Stakeholder notifications on customer's behalf
- Monthly compliance calls
- Escalation management

**What customer does:**
- Physical inventory actions
- Approvals (we prepare, they approve)

**Best for:** Facilities wanting end-to-end recall management support

**Margin profile:** ~60% (regular human touchpoints)

---

#### Tier 3: RMaaS Complete ($2,499/month)

**What we do:**
Everything in Core, plus:
- Dedicated recall specialist assigned
- Real-time response coordination
- Vendor/manufacturer communication
- Patient notification coordination (for implants)
- Regulatory reporting assistance
- On-site audit support (virtual or in-person)

**What customer does:**
- Physical actions only
- Executive approvals

**Best for:** Complex facilities, those with compliance concerns, or during transitions

**Margin profile:** ~50% (dedicated human resources)
