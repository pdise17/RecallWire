# RecallWire Pricing Strategy Recommendations

## Executive Summary

RecallWire's pricing strategy is built on a **simplified two-tier model** designed for clarity, competitive positioning, and sustainable growth:

- **Standard Tier** ($4,999/year): 1-5 locations, competing with PAR Excellence
- **Enterprise Tier** (Starting at $25,000/year): 6+ locations, competing with Inmar OneRecall and ECRI

**Key Principle:** Be transparent on pricing while positioning as a premium alternative to budget competitors and a value alternative to enterprise incumbents.

## Detailed Tier Specifications

### Tier 1: Standard ($4,999/year or $449/month)

**Target Customer:**
- Single-facility clinics and hospitals
- Ambulatory Surgery Centers (ASCs)
- Multi-site practices (2-5 locations)
- Small community hospitals
- Specialty clinic networks

**Included Features:**
- Real-time FDA recall alerts (medical devices + drugs)
- Advanced inventory matching (CSV + basic API)
- Multi-facility hierarchy (up to 5 locations)
- Cross-facility reporting
- Department routing (basic)
- Guided resolution workflow
- Complete audit trail and compliance documentation
- RecallWire Assistant (500 queries/month)
- Priority email support (24hr response)
- Self-serve onboarding with onboarding call

**Excluded Features:**
- Phone support
- Dedicated CSM
- Custom workflows
- SSO
- Premium security reviews
- Sandbox environments

**Operational Model:**
- Self-service with guided onboarding
- No dedicated CSM
- Priority email support
- Automated billing (credit card or invoice)

**Competitive Positioning:**

| Dimension | RecallWire Standard | PAR Excellence |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------|
| Price | $4,999/yr | ~$2,400/yr (est.) |
| AI capabilities | Full Assistant | None |
| Recall action tracking | Complete workflow | Notification only |
| Role-based sign-offs | Yes | No |
| Premium | +108% | Baseline |

**Value Justification:** RecallWire is 2x PAR's price but delivers complete recall lifecycle management with AI-powered guidance. For an extra $200/month, customers get comprehensive compliance coverage that PAR's notification-only approach cannot provide.

## Pricing Rationale

### Why Two Tiers?

| Problem with 4 Tiers | Solution with 2 Tiers |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| Buyer confusion ("Which tier?") | Clear SMB vs Enterprise choice |
| Sales paralysis on tier recommendation | Instant qualification |
| Complex feature gates | Simple feature differentiation |
| Pricing page overwhelm | Clean, scannable comparison |

### Unit Economics by Tier

| Tier | Revenue | Est. CAC | Est. Support Cost | Est. Margin |
|------|---------|----------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Standard** | $4,999/yr | $800 (PLG/hybrid) | $500/yr | 70%+ |
| **Enterprise** | $25,000+/yr | $6,000-10,000 | $3,000-5,000/yr | 60-65% |

### Competitive Positioning Summary

| Competitor | Their Positioning | RecallWire Advantage |
|------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Inmar OneRecall** | Enterprise-only, opaque, 60% market share | Transparent pricing, modern AI, challenger energy |
| **ECRI Alerts** | Premium ($76K), trusted brand | 1/3 the cost, AI-powered, comparable coverage |
| **PAR Excellence** | Budget option (~$2.4K), basic features | Complete workflow, AI guidance, premium quality |

## Go-to-Market Considerations

### Standard Tier (PLG + Hybrid Motion)

- **Acquisition**: SEO, content marketing, targeted outreach
- **Conversion**: Free trial → demo → sales-assisted close
- **Success Metrics**: Trial-to-paid conversion, time to value
- **Key Investment**: Onboarding UX, self-service tools

### Enterprise Tier (Sales-Led Motion)

- **Acquisition**: Account-based marketing, industry events, referrals
- **Conversion**: Discovery → demo → pilot → contract
- **Success Metrics**: Enterprise pipeline, deal size, retention
- **Key Investment**: Enterprise sales team, CSM capacity, implementation services

## Risk Considerations

### Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Standard perceived as "cheap"** | Premium pricing (+108% vs PAR), quality messaging |
| **Enterprise cannibalization** | Clear feature gates (SSO, custom workflows, dedicated support) |
| **Support overwhelm** | Self-service first, automated onboarding |
| **PAR price matching** | Feature differentiation (AI, complete workflow) |
| **Inmar/ECRI response** | First-mover on transparency, modern UX advantage |

---

## Summary Recommendation

1. **Launch 2-tier structure** with transparent public pricing
2. **Position Standard at $4,999/year** as premium alternative to PAR
3. **Position Enterprise at "Starting at $25,000"** as value alternative to ECRI
4. **Invest in self-service** to make Standard tier sustainable
5. **Use category add-ons** to increase ARPU without tier complexity
6. **Maintain internal sub-tiers** for Enterprise sales flexibility
