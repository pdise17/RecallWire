## Part 4: RecallWire vs. Competitors

### RecallWire vs. PAR Excellence/NotiSphere

**PAR Excellence positioning:** Budget solution at ~$200/month

#### The Critical Gap: Task Tracking vs. Recall Action Tracking

Based on competitive analysis, PAR Excellence tracks at the **task level** (communication status), but does NOT track **Recall Actions** (what you do with affected inventory after identification).

**What PAR Excellence tracks (Task Level):**
- Did you receive the notification? ✓
- Did you acknowledge it? ✓
- Did you respond to the manufacturer? ✓

**What PAR Excellence does NOT track (Recall Action Level):**
- What did you DO with the affected inventory?
- Quarantine status and location
- Return to manufacturer (with shipping documentation)
- Destruction/disposal (with verification)
- Correction/repair actions
- Patient notification (for implanted devices)
- Sign-offs and approvals at each step

#### Why This Matters: FDA-Defined Recall Actions

Per [FDA 21 CFR Part 806](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/recalls-corrections-and-removals-devices), healthcare facilities must execute and document specific recall actions:

| FDA Recall Action | Definition | Documentation Required |
|-------------------|------------|----------------------|
| **Quarantine** | Segregate affected product from usable inventory | Location, quantity, date, who |
| **Return** | Physical removal to manufacturer | Shipping records, quantities, dates |
| **Destroy** | Disposal of affected product | Destruction verification, witness |
| **Correct** | Repair, modify, relabel, or inspect in place | Action taken, by whom, verification |
| **Patient Monitoring** | For implants: identify and monitor affected patients | Patient list, notification records, follow-up |

> "A request for termination of a recall order shall include **a description of the disposition of the recalled device**."
> — [FDA 21 CFR Part 810](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-H/part-810)

#### Comparison: What Each Platform Documents

| When Auditor Asks... | PAR Excellence Answer | RecallWire Answer |
|---------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| "Did you receive notice of Recall X?" | "Yes, acknowledged 1/15" | "Yes, acknowledged 1/15" |
| "What did you do with affected inventory?" | "We responded to manufacturer" | "Quarantined 47 units on 1/15, returned 42 to manufacturer on 1/20 (tracking #xyz), destroyed 5 expired units on 1/22" |
| "Who performed these actions?" | ??? | "John Smith (Materials), approved by Jane Doe (Manager)" |
| "Can you show me the documentation?" | Email chain, maybe | Complete audit trail with timestamps, photos, sign-offs |
| "How do I know this was done correctly?" | ??? | "Here's the guided workflow we followed, with each step documented" |

## Part 5: ROI Calculator Framework

### Input Variables

| Variable | Description | Default |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| `locations` | Number of facilities | - |
| `annual_alerts` | Estimated alerts received/year | 500 × locations |
| `fte_hourly_cost` | Fully loaded hourly rate | $50 |
| `current_fte` | FTEs currently doing recall work | 0.1 × locations |
| `audit_prep_hours` | Hours spent on audit prep/year | 40 |
| `current_platform_cost` | Existing solution cost/year | $0 |

### ROI Calculation

```
Manual Cost = (current_fte × 2,080 × fte_hourly_cost) +
              (audit_prep_hours × fte_hourly_cost) +
              risk_exposure

Platform Cost = recallwire_subscription +
                (reduced_fte × 2,080 × fte_hourly_cost) +
                (reduced_audit_hours × fte_hourly_cost) +
                reduced_risk_exposure

Annual Savings = Manual Cost - Platform Cost

ROI = (Annual Savings / recallwire_subscription) × 100

Payback (days) = (recallwire_subscription / Annual Savings) × 365
```
