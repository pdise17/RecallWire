# RecallWire Simplified Pricing Model

## Overview

This document outlines a streamlined two-tier pricing structure designed for clarity and competitive positioning:

1. **Standard Tier** - Published pricing for 1-5 locations (competing with PAR Excellence)
2. **Enterprise Tier** - Published starting price for 6+ locations (competing with Inmar OneRecall and ECRI)

## Usage Limits & Fair Use

### Per-Tier Usage Allowances

| Usage Metric | Standard | Enterprise | Enterprise Plus | Enterprise Premier |
|--------------|----------|------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Alerts processed/mo** | 2,500 | 10,000 | 25,000 | 50,000+ |
| **AI queries/mo** | 500 | 2,000 | 5,000 | Unlimited |
| **Document scans/mo** | 500 | 2,000 | 5,000 | 10,000+ |
| **API calls/mo** | 10,000 | 50,000 | 100,000 | Unlimited |
| **Data retention** | 3 years | 5 years | 7 years | Custom |
| **Storage (GB)** | 25 | 100 | 250 | Custom |

### Overage Handling

**Approach: Soft limits with relationship-based conversations**

1. Alert customer at 80% of limit
2. Allow overage up to 150% of limit
3. CSM outreach for sustained overages
4. Recommend tier upgrade or negotiate custom terms

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't hard-block access at threshold
- Don't send automated overage billing
- Don't audit customers aggressively
