## Customized Intro Email

**Subject:** Recall management across 19 hospitals—where Prisma's supply chain excellence can go next

---

Hi [Name],

Prisma Health's supply chain transformation has been impressive—$450M in margin improvement, the centralized MDC, and now the 2025 ECRI Health Technology Excellence Award. That kind of operational discipline is exactly why recall management stands out as a gap worth closing.

Across 19 hospitals and 3,131 beds, your team likely processes thousands of FDA recall alerts annually. The challenge isn't the critical ones—it's the 95% that don't apply but still require manual verification. That triage burden multiplies across locations, and when a real Class I recall hits, proving coordinated response across the Midlands and Upstate can consume dozens of hours.

RecallWire automates the match between FDA alerts and your actual inventory—by lot, serial, and location—so your team only sees what's relevant. The platform routes notifications to the right contacts at each facility, tracks response through quarantine and disposition, and maintains the audit documentation Joint Commission and CMS require.

Given Prisma's focus on supply chain technology (I noticed you're using Premier's PINC AI suite), this may fit naturally into your existing infrastructure. We integrate with ERP and inventory systems and can work alongside your current tools.

Would 15 minutes work to compare how recall triage currently flows across your facilities and where automation can reduce that load?

Best,
[Name]

## Why This Version Works

### 1. Opens with Their Success (Not Your Product)
References the $450M transformation, MDC, and ECRI award—shows you've done homework and positions recall management as the *next* optimization, not a criticism.

### 2. Specific Scale
"19 hospitals and 3,131 beds" is more credible than "hospital networks like Prisma Health."

### 3. Acknowledges Their Tech Stack
Mentioning PINC AI shows you understand their environment and positions RecallWire as complementary, not competitive with their existing investments.

### 4. Geographic Specificity
"Midlands and Upstate" signals you know their footprint spans regions (Columbia/Greenville).

### 5. Compliance Stakes (Implicit)
"Joint Commission and CMS require" is more sophisticated than listing penalty amounts—assumes they know the stakes.

### 6. Natural CTA
Frames the call as comparing current state vs. automation potential, not a product demo.

## Notes on ECRI

Prisma won the 2025 ECRI Health Technology Excellence Award. This likely means they have a relationship with ECRI and may use **ECRI Alerts** for recall notifications.

From your competitive research, ECRI's weaknesses are:
- Legacy technology (nonprofit model, slower innovation)
- Bundled pricing with other ECRI services
- Alert service only—no workflow automation or disposition tracking

If they're using ECRI Alerts, the angle is: *"ECRI is great for early notification, but RecallWire handles what happens next—matching to inventory, routing, tracking response, and audit documentation."*

You could probe this on the call: "Are you currently using ECRI Alerts or another service for recall notifications?"
