## Why This Works

1. **Short** — respects their time and the medium
2. **Opens with them** — references their award, not your product
3. **Asks a question** — invites dialogue vs. pitching
4. **No product name** — "recall management automation" is curiosity-inducing
5. **Low pressure** — "no pitch, just curious" disarms
