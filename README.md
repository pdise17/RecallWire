## Key Metrics

### Pricing Summary (Current 2-Tier Model)

| Tier | Annual | Target |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Standard** | $4,999/year | 1-5 locations |
| **Enterprise** | Starting at $25,000/year | 6+ locations |

**Add-Ons:** Category modules ($799-$1,499/yr), RMaaS managed services ($499-$2,499/mo)

### Market Intelligence

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Top 3 GPOs market share | ~80% |
| Annual FDA recalls (2024) | ~1,080 events |
| Class I recalls (2024) | 75+ (15-year high) |
| Software-related recalls | 35% of total |

### Competitive Position

| Competitor | Market Position | RecallWire Advantage |
|------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| ECRI | Market leader | Modern UX, transparent pricing |
| Inmar OneRecall | 60% of hospitals | Full device coverage (not just pharmacy) |
| PAR Excellence | Budget option | Recall Action tracking (not just tasks) |
