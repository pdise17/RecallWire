The Problem: Recall Volume Is Accelerating — Across Both Categories

Healthcare facilities face recall exposure across two critical domains. Most still manage both with spreadsheets, inboxes, and manual cross-referencing. The volume is unsustainable — and the compliance stakes have never been higher.

Medical Device Recalls
  • 2,812+ device recalls in 2025 — unprecedented volume
  • 75+ Class I recalls in 2024 — 15-year high since 2009
  • 11.1% caused by device failures — highest rate in 5+ years
  • FDA processing lags 25–45 days behind real-time manufacturer actions
  • UDI matching across complex multi-site inventories is still largely manual
  • Joint Commission requires documented response at every facility
Pharmaceutical Recalls
  • FDA issues hundreds of drug recalls annually — Class I through III
  • DSCSA compliance requires lot-level drug traceability across the supply chain
  • NDC and lot number matching across formularies remains largely manual
  • Pharmacy teams receive alerts via separate channels — no unified recall view
  • Contamination, mislabeling, and counterfeit events demand immediate, documented response
  • Documentation required for DEA, state boards, and accreditation bodies
⚠ The Common Thread
Whether it's a recalled infusion pump or a contaminated injectable — the failure is the same: manual processes, fragmented alerts, and documentation that can't survive an audit. A Materials Manager or Director of Pharmacy spends 20–40 hours on a single significant recall event. RecallWire eliminates that entirely.
