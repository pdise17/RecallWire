The Problem: Recalls Are Accelerating Across Both Categories

Healthcare facilities face recall exposure across two critical domains — medical devices and pharmaceuticals. Most facilities manage both with the same broken process: inboxes, spreadsheets, and manual cross-referencing. The volume is unsustainable.

Medical Device Recalls
  • 2,812+ device recalls in 2025 — unprecedented volume
  • 75+ Class I recalls in 2024 — 15-year high
  • 11.1% caused by device failures — highest rate in 5+ years
  • FDA processing lags 25–45 days behind real-time manufacturer actions
  • UDI matching required across complex multi-site inventories
  • Joint Commission requires documented recall response at every facility
Pharmaceutical Recalls
  • FDA issues hundreds of drug recalls annually — Class I through III
  • DSCSA compliance requires lot-level drug traceability across the supply chain
  • NDC / lot number matching across formularies is still largely manual
  • Pharmacy teams alerted via separate channels — no unified recall view
  • High-profile events (contamination, mislabeling, counterfeiting) demand rapid response
  • Recall documentation required for DEA, state boards, and accreditation bodies
⚠ The Common Thread
Whether it's a recalled infusion pump or a contaminated injectable — the underlying failure is the same: manual processes, fragmented alerts, and documentation that can't survive an audit. A Materials Manager or Director of Pharmacy spends 20–40 hours on a single significant recall event. RecallWire eliminates that entirely.
