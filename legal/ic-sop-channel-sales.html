⚠ Commission Contingency — No Client Payment, No Commission
No commission is owed if RecallWire does not collect cleared payment from the client. Commission obligations arise only upon actual receipt of client funds. This applies to disputed charges, chargebacks, and any other non-payment event.
Who We Are Looking For

RecallWire is building a channel of VP-level business development professionals with established books of business in healthcare. We are not hiring generalists. We are seeking operators who already have trusted relationships inside health systems, IDNs, and large care facilities — and who can open doors based on their existing credibility.

Experience Range 5–30 years of B2B healthcare sales with a verifiable record of exceeding multi-million dollar quotas
Industry Background Medical Device sales preferred; Pharma, Supply Chain software, or Health-SaaS highly valued
Existing Network Established relationships within regional or national health systems; existing book of business
Communication Exceptional verbal and written skills; able to lead C-suite discovery independently
Tools Own professional toolset; active LinkedIn presence required minimum
Operating Style True independent operator — minimal hand-holding, high credibility, self-directed
Role & Responsibilities
What the IC Does Contractor
  1. Prospect — Identify and research target accounts within agreed territory or vertical.
  2. Outreach — Conduct outbound BD using professional network, LinkedIn, phone, and email.
  3. Qualify — Run initial discovery to assess fit and understand recall management pain points.
  4. Set Appointments — Book qualified demo appointments for RecallWire Co-Founders. This is the primary deliverable.
  5. Report — Maintain pipeline visibility and share market feedback at weekly meetings.
  6. Attend Meetings — Two 1-hour virtual meetings per week (Tue & Fri).
  7. Manage Relationships — Maintain ongoing client relationships at a high-touch level through periodic check-ins focused on relationship continuity; day-to-day account management is not expected.
What RecallWire Does Company
  1. Demos & Closing — Co-Founders lead all product demos, discovery calls, procurement discussions, and contract negotiations. The IC attends initial client cycles in a supporting capacity; these are critical learning cycles for the founding team. As the program matures, expanded IC involvement will be evaluated.
  2. Collateral — Provide sales materials, one-pagers, pitch decks, battlecards, and competitive positioning.
  3. Deal Support — Available for warm handoffs and executive-level calls as needed.
  4. Commission Payout — Paid within 3–5 business days of cleared client payment received. ACH preferred; international and other payment types accommodated. No upfront commissions — cleared client payment is required before any commission is released.

Payment Mechanics

  • One-time payment — Commission is a single lump-sum; it is not distributed monthly over the contract term.
  • Net collection basis — Paid 3–5 business days after cleared client payment is received. No upfront commissions under any circumstances.
  • Payment method — ACH preferred; wire, check, and international payments accommodated.
  • High-volume cadence — If deal volume warrants, payout schedule can shift to monthly batch payments by mutual agreement.
Vendor & Distributor Partnerships — Track 2

In addition to direct health system sales, RecallWire is actively pursuing white label and distribution partnerships with medical device manufacturers, supply chain companies, GPOs, and distributors. These are high-leverage opportunities — a single partnership can push RecallWire's solution to hundreds of facilities through an existing trusted commercial relationship.

What a Vendor / Distributor Partnership Looks Like
A device maker or distributor packages RecallWire as part of their existing offering — either co-branded or white labeled under their name. They sell or bundle it to their existing customer base. RecallWire handles fulfillment, onboarding, and platform support. The IC who originates the relationship is compensated on the resulting deal.

IC Role in Vendor / Distributor Deals

IC Responsibilities Contractor
  1. Identify — Leverage existing relationships with vendors, distributors, or GPOs to surface partnership opportunities.
  2. Qualify — Assess whether the partner has the distribution reach and customer alignment to make a white label deal viable.
  3. Introduce — Make the warm introduction to RecallWire Co-Founders. This is the IC's primary deliverable on this track.
  4. Support — Remain available as a relationship bridge during Co-Founder-led negotiations. White label deals evolve on a case-by-case basis; the IC will have visibility into how each deal progresses.
RecallWire Handles Company
  1. Negotiation — Co-Founders lead all partnership structure, pricing, and white label agreement terms.
  2. Legal & Contracting — RecallWire drafts and executes all partner agreements.
  3. Platform & Fulfillment — RecallWire manages white label delivery, onboarding, and support for the partner's customers.
  4. Commission Payout — IC compensation is determined and documented prior to deal close; paid within 3–5 business days of cleared funds. No commission is released before cleared client payment is received.

Compensation — Vendor / Distributor Track

Compensation on vendor and distributor partnerships is negotiated on a deal-by-deal basis and is not subject to the standard 30% direct commission rate. The following factors inform IC compensation on these deals:

FactorConsideration
Deal SizeTotal contract value of the white label agreement (can range from $50K to $1M+ depending on partner reach)
IC's Role in OriginationWhether the IC sourced, introduced, and/or actively facilitated the deal through close
Partner ReachNumber of downstream facilities the partner distributes to — higher reach = higher deal value
ExclusivityWhether the arrangement includes territorial or vertical exclusivity for the partner
Agreed RateFlat fee or percentage of deal value — confirmed in writing by Co-Founders prior to introduction
Important — Agree on Compensation Before Making the Introduction
For all vendor and distributor opportunities, the IC must confirm their compensation arrangement with a RecallWire Co-Founder in writing before making the introduction. No compensation obligation exists for unconfirmed introductions. This protects both parties and ensures alignment going into every partnership conversation.
Pipeline, Lead Flow & Territory

Lead Flow

  • Self-sourced first — The immediate expectation is that the IC taps their existing book of business and professional network to generate pipeline. Warm leads from RecallWire are limited at launch.
  • Warm leads — Currently one Co-Founder manages the primary internal warm lead source. As LinkedIn and social campaigns scale, warm leads will be routed to the IC. Generating warm leads for the IC is a stated goal as the program grows.
  • Internal BD engine — RecallWire has health systems across NY, NJ, CT, SC, GA, and FL fully mapped with structured contact data. Once the IC's own network is tapped, this pipeline will be made available. Additional mass-market and social strategies in development will feed the IC's pipeline over time.

Territory

No formal territories are defined at this stage — RecallWire is an early-stage, greenfield market. Co-Founders are not competing with the IC for any accounts. The near-term priority is leveraging the IC's existing relationships to gain initial traction. As the program matures, defined territories and structured lead routing will be formalized in the full Channel Sales Agreement.

Performance Philosophy
Our Approach at This Stage
RecallWire does not focus heavily on hard metrics, especially as we start out. We are building something deliberate, not manufacturing activity for its own sake. Quality of conversations matters most.

What Matters Most

  • Quality of conversations initiated and caliber of decision-makers engaged
  • Pipeline transparency and honest, timely reporting
  • Consistent meeting attendance and follow-through
  • Real market feedback that sharpens our positioning
Public Representation Guidelines

The IC is a true independent contractor. Public representation is at the IC's discretion — the IC may choose to publicly identify as a RecallWire channel representative or staff member, or may prefer to keep their affiliation private. Either approach is acceptable and is the IC's choice.

All product and pricing representations must align with RecallWire-provided materials regardless of how the IC chooses to represent their affiliation publicly.

Path to Formal Agreement

These terms represent the starting point. If both parties find the arrangement productive, RecallWire will formalize the relationship with a full Channel Sales Agreement that may include:

  • Defined territory or vertical
  • Agreed performance benchmarks
  • Expanded commission structure (if applicable)
  • Co-branding or public representation rights (if applicable)
Next Steps
If these base terms and responsibilities make sense, we can move forward to a formalized Channel Sales Agreement with base performance metrics. To proceed, provide the required documentation listed in the Onboarding Checklist above.
