1.   Engagement & Role

RecallWire engages the Contractor as an Independent Contractor in the capacity of VP of Business Development / Channel Sales Representative on a non-exclusive basis. The Contractor is not an employee, partner, or publicly recognized staff member of RecallWire. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

RecallWire is seeking a high-caliber business development professional to spearhead market expansion into large healthcare facilities and multi-site health systems. The Contractor will lead executive-level conversations to demonstrate how RecallWire's recall management platform reduces manual steps and clinical risk.

2.   Scope of Services

The Contractor agrees to perform the following services on behalf of RecallWire:

  1. Strategic Growth. Own the front-end of the sales cycle — from initial prospecting through qualified appointment setting. Conduct outreach with the goal of booking qualified demos for RecallWire Co-Founders.
  2. Stakeholder Engagement. Navigate complex healthcare hierarchies to engage decision-makers in supply chain, clinical engineering, quality, compliance, and C-suite.
  3. Pipeline Management. Maintain accurate records of outreach activity and pipeline status, reporting on conversion metrics and market feedback during weekly meetings.
  4. Cross-Functional Partnership. Participate in required weekly virtual meetings and provide field intelligence to refine messaging and product positioning.

Primary Deliverable. At this stage, the Contractor's primary deliverable is qualified appointment setting for RecallWire Co-Founders. Closing contracts and post-sale account management are handled by RecallWire unless otherwise agreed in writing.

3.   Target Market

The Contractor shall focus outreach efforts on the following:

  • Geography: United States (regions and verticals to be determined collaboratively).
  • Primary Targets: Large care facilities, multi-site health systems, Integrated Delivery Networks (IDNs), and Academic Medical Centers.
  • Key Buyer Personas: Supply Chain Directors, Materials Managers, Risk Managers, Quality Directors, Biomedical Engineering leadership, and C-suite executives.
4.   Compensation — Commission Structure

4.1   Commission Rate

The Contractor will earn a commission of thirty percent (30%) of gross sales revenue for each client contract signed as a direct result of the Contractor's outreach and appointment-setting activity.

4.2   Commission Basis & Term

Commission is calculated on the gross sales value of the signed client contract. Payout applies to the life of the first contract only, typically a period of twelve (12) months. Subsequent contract renewals or upsells are not subject to commission under this Agreement unless a separate written amendment is executed.

4.3   Commission Triggers

A commission obligation is created only when all of the following conditions are simultaneously satisfied: (i) a client contract is fully executed by both RecallWire and the client; (ii) the Contractor is the documented source of the initial client introduction and appointment; and (iii) RecallWire has received cleared, collected payment from the client as provided in Section 4.5 below.

4.4   Payment Schedule

Commissions shall be paid within five (5) to seven (7) business days of RecallWire receiving cleared payment from the client. The Contractor is responsible for providing valid payment details prior to the first payout (see Section 9).

4.5   Contingency — No Client Payment, No Commission

RecallWire's obligation to pay commission to the Contractor is strictly contingent upon RecallWire's actual receipt of full, cleared payment from the client. If a client fails to pay, disputes a charge, initiates a chargeback, becomes insolvent, or if RecallWire does not receive collected funds for any reason whatsoever, no commission shall be owed or payable to the Contractor with respect to that contract or any unpaid portion thereof. Commission obligations arise solely to the extent that RecallWire has actually collected payment from the applicable client. This provision shall survive termination of this Agreement.

4.6   No Base Salary

This is a commission-only engagement. No base salary, draw, advances, benefits, or expense reimbursement is provided unless separately agreed upon in a signed written amendment.

5.   Performance Expectations

RecallWire does not impose rigid quantitative metrics at this stage of growth. The quality of conversations and the caliber of relationships developed are of primary importance.

5.1   Preferred Candidate Profile

  • 5 to 30 years of B2B healthcare sales experience with a verifiable record of exceeding multi-million dollar quotas.
  • Medical Device sales background strongly preferred; Pharma, Supply Chain software, or Health-SaaS experience is highly valued.
  • Exceptional verbal and written communication skills; demonstrated ability to lead discovery at the C-suite level.
  • An established book of business with existing relationships within regional or national health systems.
  • Active LinkedIn presence (required minimum tool for this role).

5.2   Conduct Standards

  • The Contractor shall represent RecallWire's product and mission honestly and professionally at all times.
  • The Contractor shall not make representations about RecallWire's product, pricing, or capabilities beyond what is documented in RecallWire-provided materials.
  • The Contractor shall not publicly identify as a RecallWire employee, officer, or staff member.
6.   Meeting Requirements

The Contractor shall participate in two (2) one-hour virtual meetings per week with RecallWire Co-Founders, scheduled on Tuesdays and Fridays. The purpose of these meetings is pipeline review, strategy alignment, field feedback exchange, and deal support coordination.

Missed meetings must be communicated at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance. Consistent unavailability without notice may constitute grounds for termination of this Agreement.

7.   Tools & Resources

The Contractor is expected to utilize their own professional toolset for business development activities, including but not limited to CRM, email and phone outreach platforms, and LinkedIn (required minimum). RecallWire will provide the Contractor with sales collateral, battlecards, pitch decks, and Co-Founder availability for deal support. RecallWire is open to providing additional tools upon mutual written agreement.

8.   Independent Contractor Status

The Contractor is, and shall remain, an independent contractor. RecallWire shall not withhold income taxes or payroll taxes from any payments made hereunder, nor shall RecallWire provide workers' compensation, unemployment insurance, health benefits, or any other employee benefit. RecallWire shall not direct or control the Contractor's day-to-day methods of work or schedule, except as expressly required by this Agreement.

The Contractor is solely responsible for all applicable federal, state, and local taxes on compensation received under this Agreement. RecallWire shall issue a Form 1099-NEC (or equivalent) where required by applicable law. The Contractor may not subcontract or assign their obligations under this Agreement without prior written consent from RecallWire.

9.   Payment Information

Prior to the first commission disbursement, the Contractor must provide RecallWire with the following information through a secure channel designated by RecallWire:

  • Preferred payment method (Wise, Airwallex, ACH, or Wire);
  • Full account and routing details as required by the selected method;
  • Tax identification number (SSN for individuals, EIN for U.S. entities, BSN or equivalent for international contractors); and
  • Completed IRS Form W-9 or Form W-8BEN, as applicable.
10.   Confidentiality

The Contractor agrees to keep all RecallWire business information, client lists, prospect data, pricing, product details, and proprietary materials strictly confidential; not to disclose, use, or share such information outside of this engagement without the prior written consent of RecallWire; and to return or permanently destroy all confidential materials promptly upon termination of this Agreement. This obligation shall survive termination for a period of two (2) years.

11.   Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following termination for any reason, the Contractor agrees not to directly solicit, divert, or accept business from RecallWire's existing clients or identified prospects (those introduced to or identified by the Contractor during the term hereof) for competing recall management products or services.

12.   Intellectual Property

Any sales materials, prospect lists, written communications, reports, or other work product created specifically for RecallWire under this Agreement shall be considered works made for hire and shall remain the sole property of RecallWire. The Contractor retains ownership of all pre-existing tools, templates, and methodologies.

13.   Term & Termination

This Agreement commences on the date first written above and continues until terminated by either Party. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party. Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice in the event of a material breach of this Agreement by the other Party that remains uncured for ten (10) days following notice of such breach.

Upon termination, commissions earned on client contracts that were both signed and for which cleared payment was received by RecallWire prior to the effective date of termination shall be paid in accordance with Section 4.

14.   Limitation of Liability

RecallWire's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total commissions actually paid to the Contractor in the three (3) calendar months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the applicable claim. In no event shall either Party be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages, regardless of the form of the claim or the theory of liability.

15.   Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between the Parties. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association in accordance with its Commercial Arbitration Rules.

16.   Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous discussions, representations, and agreements, whether oral or written. This Agreement may not be amended except by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

17.   Required Contractor Documentation

Prior to execution and first commission disbursement, the Contractor must deliver the following to RecallWire:

  • Entity type declaration: Individual or Corporation / LLC
  • If Corporation / LLC: Articles of Incorporation or official state formation documents
  • If Individual: Current mailing address
  • Driver's License: Clear copy, front and back
  • Payment & banking details: Wise, Airwallex, or ACH information
  • Tax identification: SSN, EIN, BSN, or equivalent
  • Completed W-9 or W-8BEN as applicable
