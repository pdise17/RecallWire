## Key Trends

1. **Consolidation**: Large companies continue to acquire smaller innovators (e.g., J&J acquiring Shockwave for $13B)
2. **Robotics Growth**: Intuitive Surgical's dominance driving investment in surgical robotics
3. **Diagnostics/Digital Health**: Growing segment with companies like Dexcom, iRhythm
4. **China Growth**: Mindray and MicroPort represent growing Chinese medtech presence
