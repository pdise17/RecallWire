# Medical Device Vendors Ranked by FDA Recalls (2020-2024)

## Overview

This document ranks medical device manufacturers by the number of FDA recall events over the past 5 years (2020-2024). Data is compiled from FDA databases, industry reports, and news sources.

**Important Note**: The number of recalls does not necessarily indicate poor quality - larger companies with more products naturally have more recall exposure. Additionally, proactive recalls demonstrate responsible quality management.

## Key Statistics (2020-2024)

| Year | Total Recall Events | Class I Recalls | Trend |
|------|-------------------|-----------------|-------|

| 2022 | ~920 | 70 (15-yr high) | +2% |

## Root Causes of Recalls (2020-2024)

| Root Cause | % of Recalls | Common Examples |
|------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Software issues | 35% | Display errors, calculation bugs, connectivity |
| Manufacturing defects | 22% | Component failures, contamination, assembly |
| Design issues | 18% | Material selection, structural failure |
| Labeling errors | 12% | Wrong instructions, missing warnings |
| Quality control | 8% | Inspection escapes, process deviation |
| Other | 5% | Packaging, sterility, supply chain |

Source: [UL Solutions - Medical Device Recall Trends 2024](https://www.ul.com/insights/medical-device-recalls-key-trends-2024)

## Implications for RecallWire

1. **Target Customer Profile**: The top 20 companies by recall activity represent the highest-volume recall management needs
2. **Industry Pain Points**: Software-related recalls (35%) suggest opportunity for digital recall management solutions
3. **Concentration Risk**: A handful of mega-recalls (Philips, BD Alaris) can overwhelm hospital recall processes
4. **Proactive vs Reactive**: Companies with robust recall management may have higher reported recalls due to proactive identification
