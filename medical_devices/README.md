## Key Insights

1. **Market concentration**: Top 14 companies (>$10B revenue) control ~50% of market
2. **Recall correlation**: Largest companies have most recalls (portfolio size effect)
3. **Philips dominance**: Respironics mega-recall (15-18M units) skews data
4. **Software rising**: 35% of recalls now software-related (up from 25%)
5. **GPO power**: Top 3 GPOs control ~80% of hospital purchasing
